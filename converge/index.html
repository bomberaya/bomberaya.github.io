Converge

A marble timing puzzle for iPhone, iPad and Android

Colored tracks wind down a board, each with a steel marble at its top. Tap the marbles to launch them, and land them all in the tray together before its gate shuts. Every marble plays its own note, so a perfect landing rings out as a chord.
