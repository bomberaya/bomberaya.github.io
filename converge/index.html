Converge

Tap in time, land together. A marble timing puzzle for iPhone, iPad and Android.

Coming soon to the App Store for iPhone and iPad.

Converge is a calm, clever timing puzzle. Colored tracks wind down a board, each with a steel marble at its top. Tap the marbles to launch them, and land them all in the tray together before its gate shuts. Every marble plays its own note, so a perfect landing rings out as a chord.

A new puzzle every day

Every day brings one daily puzzle, the same for everyone. Solve it, keep your streak going and share your result. Got a result from a friend? Open today's daily in the game and try to beat it.

In the game

  • Endless levels, from two tracks up to six
  • Up to three stars on every level, and a Score that grows with them
  • Famous classical pieces to unlock: hear your marbles play them, and guess the tune
  • Leaderboards on Game Center and Google Play Games
  • Stuck? The hint plays the solving rhythm, as music or as flashing tracks
  • Every level can be solved, and it's the same level on every device
  • Plays offline

Free, with an ad between levels now and then. A one-time purchase removes ads for good.
