Converge Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Converge is a marble timing puzzle for iPhone, iPad and Android, made by Bojan Radivojevic, an independent developer in Belgrade, Serbia. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

In short

  • The game has no accounts and doesn't ask who you are.
  • Your progress and settings stay on your device.
  • The free game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device information to show and measure those ads, as described below.
  • Purchases are handled by the App Store or Google Play. I never see your payment details.
  • I don't sell your information.

Information the game keeps on your device

Converge saves your current level, the levels you have cleared, your sound and vibration settings, and whether you have removed ads. This stays on your device and is never sent to me. On Android, it may be included in your device's own backup, which Google stores for you under your Google account.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version of Converge shows full-screen ads between levels, served by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and use information such as:

  • your device's advertising identifier (the Android advertising ID, or Apple's IDFA if you allow tracking),
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information, such as the device model, operating system, language and app version,
  • how you interact with ads, such as views and taps.

Google uses this information under its own privacy policy. You can read how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Your choices about ads

  • Consent: in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, and wherever the law requires it, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before showing ads. You can change your choice at any time in the game: open the menu (top-left button) and tap Privacy choices.
  • Tracking on iPhone and iPad: the game asks for permission to track. If you don't allow it, Google doesn't receive your advertising identifier and ads are not personalized. You can change this in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on the device).
  • Remove ads: a one-time purchase in the menu turns ads off for good. After that, the game doesn't request ads.

Purchases

"Remove ads" is bought through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google processes the payment under their own terms and privacy policies. I don't receive your name, email or payment details. The game asks the store whether you own the purchase, so it can restore it after a reinstall or on a new device.

Children

Converge is not directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Keeping and deleting information

Deleting the app removes everything it saved on your device. "Start over from level 1" in the menu erases your progress. I don't hold any personal information about you, so there is nothing for me to delete; for information Google collected for ads, see Google's privacy policy linked above.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information about you, or to object to how it is used. Because the game doesn't send me any personal information, most requests about ad data are handled by Google. You can still contact me with any question or request, and I will help.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
